## workflow_map
### Item 1
- **stage**: 1) Lead intake and qualification
- **owner_roles**: ['Producer/Account Executive (AE)', 'CSR/Account Manager (AM)', 'Sales Ops/CRM admin']
- **current_process**: Inbound lead via phone/email/website. Producer/CSR captures basic risk/exposure info. Data is entered into CRM/sales tool and then into AMS; for PL, often prepped for comparative rating; for CL, prepped for data-gathering packet (ACORDs, supplementals).
- **common_tools**: ['AgencyZoom or similar sales pipeline tool integrated with AMS (Vertafore AgencyZoom)', 'AMS (Epic/AMS360/HawkSoft)', 'Email/phone/web forms']
- **manual_steps**: ['Double entry from CRM/email into AMS and rating tools', 'Manual creation of client/organization records and contact roles in AMS', 'Manual tasking/follow-up setup for next steps']
- **pain_points**: ['Duplicate data entry and scattered intake notes lead to later rework in quoting and submissions', 'Producers vary in completeness of initial data capture, causing back-and-forth with AMs']
- **why_painful**: Fragmented capture creates downstream rekey and missing fields when rating and building submissions; AMs must circle back to clients or producers.
- **business_impact**: AMs/CSRs spend a material share of time on ‘collection, processing and distribution of information,’ with a balance on paperwork/computer input (Insurance Journal). Early misses cascade into longer quote cycles.
### Item 2
- **stage**: 2) Risk profiling & data gathering (applications, schedules, loss runs)
- **owner_roles**: ['Account Manager/CSR (primary)', 'Producer/AE (reviews, fills gaps)']
- **current_process**: AM prepares ACORD 125/126/140/130 and carrier supplemental forms. Collects SOVs, driver/vehicle lists, payroll/class codes, COPE data. Requests loss runs from incumbent carriers. For some accounts, uses digital app portals to pre-fill and request e-signatures.
- **common_tools**: ['ACORD forms (125/126/140/130)', 'Email + PDFs; e-sign (DocuSign)', 'Indio digital submissions/forms (Applied/Indio; IVANS press)', 'Loss Run Pro or manual carrier requests', 'AMS document storage']
- **manual_steps**: ['Chasing insureds for signatures and missing schedules via email/phone', 'Manually populating ACORD/supplemental forms and rekeying into other systems', 'Requesting and tracking loss runs carrier-by-carrier']
- **pain_points**: ['Loss runs introduce delays; many states require carriers to provide loss history within about 10 business days (e.g., CA Insurance Code §11663.5 – FindLaw), but AMs still must request/track/chase', 'Redundant information across multiple forms and supplementals drives error risk and fatigue (Indio cites friction and redundant entry)']
- **why_painful**: Core underwriting inputs are fragmented across forms and emails; each missing item stalls underwriting/quoting. Loss run cycles are often the gating factor.
- **business_impact**: Days to weeks added to submission readiness when loss runs lag; staff time consumed by redundant entry and status chasing (Indio press; CA statute timing).
### Item 3
- **stage**: 3) Market selection and appetite check
- **owner_roles**: ['Marketing/Placement Specialist (in many CL shops) or AM', 'Producer/AE (smaller shops)']
- **current_process**: Identify in-appetite markets using past experience, carrier guidelines, and appetite tools. Decide between direct carriers vs. wholesalers/E&S for non-standard risks. Build the initial market list.
- **common_tools**: ['IVANS Markets/Appetite (Applied/IVANS resources; iireporter – ISU Network)', 'Carrier appetite guides/portals; wholesaler relationships', 'HawkSoft Submission Tracking to organize multi-carrier submissions']
- **manual_steps**: ['Manual comparison of appetites and coverage fit', 'Spreadsheet/list maintenance if AMS lacks submission tracking', 'Outreach to underwriter/marketing reps for clarifications']
- **pain_points**: ['Submitting to out-of-appetite carriers causes declines and rework', 'Scattered appetite intel and turnover at carriers waste cycles']
- **why_painful**: Without clear, up-to-date appetite matching, staff burn time sending/withdrawing submissions and explaining declines to clients.
- **business_impact**: Higher quote-cycle time and lower hit ratios; more back-and-forth with clients when remarketing is required (IVANS Markets created to address lack of automated appetite visibility).
### Item 4
- **stage**: 4) Rating & quoting (PL and small CL)
- **owner_roles**: ['Personal Lines CSR/AM', 'Commercial Lines Marketing/AM/Producer']
- **current_process**: - PL: Enter risk once in comparative rater to return multi-carrier quotes; then ‘bridge’ to carrier portal to bind. - Small CL: Use Tarmika single-entry quoting for eligible classes; otherwise rekey to each carrier portal or send full submission to underwriters.
- **common_tools**: ['EZLynx Rating Engine for PL (explicitly cites portal rekey time savings)', 'Tarmika for CL single-entry quoting (Applied/Tarmika)', 'Carrier portals, email to underwriters']
- **manual_steps**: ['Data rekey into portals not covered by rater/API', 'Uploading attachments (apps, loss runs, photos) to carrier/wholesaler portals', 'Email negotiations with underwriters on terms']
- **pain_points**: ['Multi-portal rekeying is tedious and error-prone (EZLynx explicitly: rekeying to each portal costs time)', 'Underwriter turnaround is variable; missing info or appetite mismatch leads to rework']
- **why_painful**: Inconsistent integrations and form questions across carriers multiply work; exceptions always fall back to email/phone with underwriters.
- **business_impact**: Longer cycle time; quoting staff become a throughput bottleneck during marketing seasons.
### Item 5
- **stage**: 5) Proposal creation and presentation
- **owner_roles**: ['Producer/AE (client-facing)', 'AM/Marketing (builds comparisons)']
- **current_process**: Compile quotes, endorsements, subjectivities, and exclusions. Create side-by-side comparisons and a proposal; present to insured; capture selection and approvals.
- **common_tools**: ['AMS report/merge tools; Word/PowerPoint; proposal builders', 'Email/video meeting tools']
- **manual_steps**: ['Manual coverage comparison across quotes', 'Assembling proposal artifacts and version control']
- **pain_points**: ['Coverage apples-to-apples comparison is manual and time-consuming; subtle exclusions are easy to miss']
- **why_painful**: Carrier quote formats differ widely; humans must normalize and explain differences.
- **business_impact**: AM/Producer time diverted from sales/advisory to document work; E&O exposure if gaps/exclusions are miscommunicated.
### Item 6
- **stage**: 6) Bind order, subjectivities, and binder issuance
- **owner_roles**: ['Producer/AE', 'AM/Marketing', 'Underwriter (carrier/MGA)']
- **current_process**: Confirm terms, satisfy subjectivities (e.g., inspections, financials), obtain signed apps and down payment if required. Issue an insurance binder (ACORD 75) as evidence of coverage until policy issues.
- **common_tools**: ['e-signature (DocuSign) for apps/forms', 'ACORD 75 binder form resources', 'AMS']
- **manual_steps**: ['Collecting signatures and payments', 'Coordinating final subjectivities via email/portals', 'Issuing binder and distributing to stakeholders']
- **pain_points**: ['Last-mile subjectivities and signatures can stall binds at quarter-end/renewal spikes', 'Binder language and dates must be precise to avoid disputes']
- **why_painful**: Coordination across client and carrier at speed, under time pressure.
- **business_impact**: Delays risk coverage gaps or missed effective dates for client projects or contracts.
### Item 7
- **stage**: 7) Post-bind policy issuance and downloads
- **owner_roles**: ['AM/CSR (servicing)', 'Download/Operations specialist', 'Accounting (for commission data)']
- **current_process**: Carrier issues policy; documents and data flow through IVANS Download/eDocs into AMS. Staff monitor daily, fix unmatched items in hold queues, and assign to correct client/policy. Commission/billing downloads processed by accounting.
- **common_tools**: ['IVANS Download and eDocs/messages (IVANS; Kite Technology)', 'AMS360/Epic download monitors and reports']
- **manual_steps**: ['Daily monitoring of download holds and manual assignment for unmatched items (Kite Technology)', 'Accounting reconciles commission statements vs AMS transactions; resolves mismatches (Vertafore AMS360 help)']
- **pain_points**: ['Unmatched downloads persist until a human assigns them; duplicates or mapping issues create cleanup work (Kite Technology)', 'Commission totals must be balanced before posting; exceptions require manual research (AMS360 help)']
- **why_painful**: Data elements used for auto-attach don’t always match policy records (name/number/date variances). Carrier differences and book-rolls add noise.
- **business_impact**: Recurring daily labor for operations/accounting; inaccurate attachments can hide critical docs; delays in commission posting affect revenue visibility.
### Item 8
- **stage**: 8) Policy checking and delivery
- **owner_roles**: ['AM/CSR (policy checker)', 'QA/Operations (some agencies outsource or specialize)']
- **current_process**: Compare issued policy vs. proposal/binder/quote to validate coverages, limits, forms, and endorsements. Identify discrepancies; request endorsements/corrections; deliver to client.
- **common_tools**: ['Manual checklists/spreadsheets', 'Specialized policy-check solutions (e.g., Exdion; outsourcing use cases – Resource Pro)']
- **manual_steps**: ['Line-by-line comparison of forms and endorsements across multiple PDFs/systems', 'Emailing carriers for corrections and tracking to completion']
- **pain_points**: ['Time-consuming and error-prone due to manual verification, inconsistent docs/terminology, multiple systems (Resource Pro use case; Exdion article)']
- **why_painful**: Non-standardized policy documents require human interpretation; minor misses create E&O exposure.
- **business_impact**: High skilled time consumed; rework loops for corrections; direct E&O risk if errors reach the insured uncorrected.
### Item 9
- **stage**: 9) Certificates of Insurance (COIs)
- **owner_roles**: ['Certificates specialist / CSR / AM']
- **current_process**: Receive COI request from insured or third party. Verify coverages and endorsements; issue ACORD 25; handle additional insured/waiver wording and custom requests; repeat for holder lists and updates after endorsements.
- **common_tools**: ['AMS COI module', 'ACORD 25', 'COI platforms (myCOI, Certificial)']
- **manual_steps**: ['Validating policy terms/endorsements vs requested wording', 'Editing special wording; handling rush requests; updating holders', 'Reissuing COIs after endorsements']
- **pain_points**: ['High-volume, low/no-revenue tasks strain staffing; IA Magazine cites 100 COIs/day at 2–4 minutes each equals ~3.5–7 staff hours', 'COIs cannot alter coverage; incorrect special wording creates E&O exposure (ACORD Certificates FAQ)']
- **why_painful**: Frequent, urgent requests and custom wording patterns require trained staff to avoid misrepresentation.
- **business_impact**: Material non-revenue labor; cycle-time pressure from client contracts; direct E&O risk if misworded.
### Item 10
- **stage**: 10) Endorsements and midterm changes
- **owner_roles**: ['CSR/AM (primary)']
